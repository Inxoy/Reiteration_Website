philosophy & past performances
philosophy

For me a concert is never just the playing of finished music. Every performance is a room I build together with the people in it: the speakers around them, the instruments in my hands and recordings that have travelled there from somewhere else.

Most of my sets happen in multichannel and ambisonic systems. Pre-produced spatial music meets live instruments in the middle of the dome: autoharp, acoustic guitar, a just intonation guitar and an eight-string. I play from its centre, among the audience and not in front of it.

Things are allowed to break. The music stops, my laptop crashes on purpose, I talk to the room, I cut my hair and hand it out, a bottle of red wine goes around. I don't want to illustrate grief, death and religion on a stage. I want to share them, as a ritual that everyone in the room is part of.

currently

Right now I am working on prepared electric guitar for the live context. I use RAVE by IRCAM, a neural audio model, to blur the line between me as the performer and the algorithm. In these pieces I don't just play the guitar, I embody the sound.
