Recommendations

games, music, videos …

Games, music, videos and other things I find great, with a few words on why.

music

Raphael Rogiński

Among many art forms, I love how time can be manipulated and bent. I believe this happens in Rogiński's brilliant guitar playing and arranging too. Seeing him play live twice changed the way I use the guitar myself, and now I incorporate “prepared” guitar into my playing a lot as well.

music

Kali Malone

Again, time is malleable here. She also introduced me to the pipe organ properly and made me want to use it. Ohhh, and this is where I found my fascination with temperaments as well.

music

Still House Plants

Time is malleable AGAIN… but this time in a groovy sense, with things twisting and turning. The way Jess, Finlay and David interlock is magical. The grooviest music, I always want to dance to it.

game · 2019

Disco Elysium

by ZA/UM

Maybe one of THE greatest artworks created in any medium, it's that good. Harry Du Bois and his plethora of voices now live inside my head too.

I NEED YOU. YOU CAN KEEP ME ON THIS EARTH. BE VIGILANT. I LOVE YOU.

performance inspiration

Lingua Ignota

She introduced me to the Sacred Harp tradition and its amazing songs. Maybe nothing on this list is as harrowing as her musical work. Unfortunately I missed her live show in Berlin because of y’know who.

performance inspiration

Otay:onii

Seeing her roll and move gracefully around me at MS Stubnitz, with at most ten other people in the audience, opened my eyes to how I NEED to perform as well. No barrier between artist and audience, the abolition of the stage… I will always cherish this experience as a formative one.

film · 1969

The Color of Pomegranates

by Sergei Parajanov

Hypnotic… the sounds… the shots… I have watched it about ten times already, and countless more times just to fall asleep to it and have vivid dreams.

book · 1973

Gravity's Rainbow

by Thomas Pynchon

What a monolith. The most well-written prose, washing over me in streams of varying degrees of disgusting and pleasurable elements.
