Mariia III

album
the album

Mariia III is an album for three pipe organs and my voice. It takes old folk songs and reinterprets them: melodies that have been passed on from mouth to mouth for centuries, sung again between instruments that were built for churches.

It is inspired by Andrei Tarkovsky's Andrei Rublev.
